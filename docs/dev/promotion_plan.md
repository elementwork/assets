## 1. Positioning

**One-liner:** *"The estate binder your family can actually open — even without an app, an internet connection, or you."*

**The wedge problem (why people buy):**
1. **Death/incapacity creates a discovery problem** — spouses/executors inherit scattered accounts, unknown subscriptions, unmapped crypto, incomplete beneficiary information and unclear document locations.
2. **Generic apps are online and often US-centric** — many inventory/net-worth tools store data in the cloud and do not reflect Canadian/Ontario household structures.
3. **Existing estate binders are static** — lawyers and planners often provide forms; EstateON is a living, editable, self-contained file that can be maintained and re-printed.

**Current positioning statement:**
> For Canadian families and their trusted professionals who want one private, portable file that documents what exists, where it is and how family can find the next step, EstateON combines a structured asset inventory, continuity fields, Emergency Access Guide, Master Asset Index and advanced readiness review without requiring a cloud account.

**Brand pillars:** Private by design · Family-ready · Portable · Practical · Canada-aware.

## 3. What we're actually selling

**Not a net-worth tracker — a family asset inventory and estate continuity system.**

- **The artifact:** one self-contained `.html` file that is both inventory and application.
- **The continuity promise:** family/POA/executor can understand what exists and where to start when the owner cannot explain it personally.
- **The trust promise:** no required account, no forced cloud, user owns the artifact.
- **The outputs:** family-ready Estate Binder, Emergency Access Guide, Master Asset Index; advanced exports in Professional.
- **The intelligence:** Access Readiness, Audit and Annual Review provide the basis for a future continuity rules engine.

**Value props to lead with:**
1. *One private file. No required account. No forced cloud.*
2. *A will says who receives assets; EstateON helps your family find them.*
3. *A structured catalog helps you remember assets a blank spreadsheet does not.*
4. *Turn the inventory into a family-ready Estate Binder.*
5. *English and Chinese are first-class product experiences.*

## 5. Pricing & packaging — current working hypothesis

The canonical rationale and packaging rules are in `product_strategy.md`.

| Edition | Working price | Primary value |
|--------|---------------|---------------|
| **Free** | CAD $0 | Discover breadth; begin a real inventory; experience the private offline model |
| **Family** | CAD $29 one-time | Turn the inventory into a family-ready continuity binder; broader organization tools |
| **Professional** | CAD $99 one-time | Audit, Access Readiness, Annual Review, advanced complexity and professional exports |
| **Optional Updates** | CAD $19/year | New taxonomy/rules/templates/product updates; not required to keep the purchased file usable |

**Principles:**
- Purchased household files should remain usable; recurring revenue must not come from holding the user’s data hostage.
- Family should be the likely volume paid edition.
- Professional must earn its premium through intelligence and complexity handling, not by making Family inconvenient.
- Free is a trust funnel and should be genuinely useful.
- Pricing remains a hypothesis until validated with conversion/refund/activation data.

## 6. Channels & campaigns

### 6.1 Organic / product-led
- **Landing page + free download** — central funnel hub; the downloadable Free artifact is itself a marketing engine.
- **SEO** — executor preparedness, aging parents, estate inventory, offline privacy, Canadian account/estate organization, digital/crypto continuity.
- **YouTube** — product walkthroughs and educational continuity content.
- **Reddit/community forums** — useful educational posts where self-promotion rules permit.
- **Mandarin channels** — WeChat/RED and Canadian Chinese-language communities with localized, non-alarmist family-organization content.

### 6.2 Paid acquisition — after PMF signals
- Google Search around high-intent estate inventory / executor / aging-parent organization terms.
- Meta/Reddit experiments for 45–70 households and adult children helping parents.
- LinkedIn experiments for lawyers/advisors/accountants only after a professional offer is clearly defined.

### 6.3 Partnerships
- Estate lawyers: structured pre-meeting inventory / client binder.
- Financial planners and insurance advisors: annual review and continuity preparation.
- Accountants: corporate/foreign asset organization and estate administration preparation.
- Community organizations: family financial-organization workshops.

Avoid partnerships that create the impression that EstateON is replacing legal/tax advice.

## 8. Funnel and conversion moments

### Acquisition
Content / professional referral / family referral → Free download.

### Activation
User identifies/edits personally relevant assets and understands that the catalog is a coverage library rather than a claim that they own every row.

### Free → Family
Primary natural triggers:
- wants Print Estate Binder;
- wants Emergency Access Guide / Master Asset Index;
- needs broader organization views for a real household.

### Family → Professional
Primary natural triggers:
- “What am I missing?”
- “How ready is this for incapacity/death?”
- complex assets/business/foreign holdings;
- annual review/audit/professional export needs.

Upgrade prompts should be contextual, not repetitive modal advertising.

## 9. Metrics & success criteria

Do not optimize only for downloads or revenue.

**Strategic north-star metric:**
> Number of households with a materially complete inventory reviewed within the last 12 months.

Useful funnel/product metrics:
- Free downloads;
- activation/completion proxy;
- Family conversion;
- Professional conversion;
- Print Estate Binder use;
- Annual Review completion;
- percentage of material assets with adequate access readiness;
- 12-month review rate;
- referral/gift rate;
- refund/support burden.

Because the core product is offline-first, analytics should remain privacy-respecting and should not require invasive telemetry inside the user’s estate file.

## 10. Risks & mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| “It’s just a spreadsheet” perception | Lead with curated taxonomy, continuity fields, Estate Binder, Access Readiness and Audit |
| Legal/tax advice liability | Educational/organizational framing; disclose assumptions; recommend professional advice for fact-dependent issues |
| Catalog count confuses users | Separate Asset Catalog / Showing / With Value / future confirmed-owned state |
| Security concerns | Clear locator-not-secret-vault guidance, encryption trade-offs, backup education |
| License tampering | Replace client-side HMAC secret model with asymmetric signature verification before relying on licensing commercially |
| Family loses encryption passphrase | Clear no-recovery warning; future recovery-design options; backup/handoff education |
| Subscription conflicts with offline trust | Keep purchased file usable; recurring revenue from optional updates/services |
| Advisor workflow overwhelms consumer product | Layer B2B2C tooling around the family-owned artifact rather than converting the core into a CRM |
